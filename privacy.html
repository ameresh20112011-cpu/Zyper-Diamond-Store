Privacy Policy

Zyper Diamond Store

1. Introduction

Zyper Diamond Store respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and how we protect it.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account, sign in, place an order, use your wallet, or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Firebase account identification information
  • Free Fire player UID and player name
  • Order and transaction information
  • Wallet balance and wallet transaction records
  • Payment receipt information submitted for verification

3. Google Sign-In

Zyper Diamond Store may allow customers to sign in using Google Authentication.

When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide us with basic account information such as your name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier.

We use this information only to create or identify your account, authenticate your login, and provide Zyper Diamond Store services.

4. Email Verification and Password Reset

Your email address may be used to send account verification codes and password reset verification codes.

These verification codes are used only for account security and identity verification.

5. How We Use Your Information

We may use information collected through Zyper Diamond Store to:

  • Create and manage your customer account
  • Authenticate your login
  • Process Free Fire top-up orders
  • Manage customer wallet balances and transactions
  • Verify customer payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized activity
  • Improve the security, reliability, and functionality of our website

6. Payment Information

Zyper Diamond Store may request payment receipts or transaction details when required to verify a wallet recharge or payment.

We do not request or store your online banking password, banking PIN, card PIN, banking OTP, or other banking login credentials.

7. Wallet Information

If you use the Zyper Diamond Store wallet, we may maintain records of your wallet balance, deposits, redeem transactions, purchases, deductions, and related transaction information.

Wallet information is used to provide wallet services, process purchases, maintain transaction history, and protect against unauthorized transactions.

8. Data Storage and Service Providers

Some information may be processed or stored using third-party infrastructure and service providers such as Firebase, Cloudflare, and other services required to operate Zyper Diamond Store.

These services may be used for authentication, database storage, security, email verification, order processing, wallet functions, and website hosting.

9. Data Sharing

Zyper Diamond Store does not sell customer personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate our services, authenticate users, process transactions, provide support, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

10. Account Security

Customers are responsible for keeping their account password, verification codes, and other account security information private.

Never share your Zyper Diamond Store password or verification code with another person.

11. Cookies and Local Storage

Zyper Diamond Store may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember settings, store selected products, improve functionality, and provide a better user experience.

12. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may interact with or link to third-party services. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms, and Zyper Diamond Store is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites or services.

13. Children's Privacy

Users should use Zyper Diamond Store only in accordance with applicable local laws and the age requirements and terms of the gaming or third-party services they use.

14. Data Accuracy

Customers should provide accurate information when creating an account, submitting an order, entering a Free Fire UID, making a wallet transaction, or contacting customer support.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website features, security practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will be made available on this page.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your account, or how your information is handled, you may contact Zyper Diamond Store through the official support options available on our website.

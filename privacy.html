Privacy Policy

Zyper Diamond Store

1. Introduction

Zyper Diamond Store respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use Zyper Diamond Store, we may collect information such as:

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Firebase account identification information
  • Free Fire player UID and player name
  • Order and transaction information
  • Wallet balance and wallet transaction records
  • Payment receipt information when submitted by the customer

3. Google Sign-In

Zyper Diamond Store may allow users to sign in using Google Authentication.

When you choose Google Sign-In, we may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier.

We use this information only for account creation, authentication, and providing Zyper Diamond Store services.

4. Email Verification and Password Reset

We may use your email address to send verification codes and password reset codes.

These codes are used only for account security and identity verification.

5. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your customer account
  • Authenticate your login
  • Process Free Fire top-up orders
  • Manage customer wallet balances
  • Verify payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud or unauthorized activity
  • Improve website security and reliability

6. Payment Information

Zyper Diamond Store may request payment receipts for verification purposes.

We do not request or store your online banking password, PIN, OTP, debit card PIN, or other banking login credentials.

7. Data Storage

Some customer information may be stored using third-party services such as Firebase and Cloudflare.

These services are used to operate authentication, order processing, wallet functions, and other website features.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell customer personal information.

Information may only be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website, process authentication, deliver services, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

9. Account Security

Customers are responsible for keeping their passwords, verification codes, and account information private.

Never share your Zyper Diamond Store password or verification code with another person.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

Zyper Diamond Store may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to keep users signed in, remember preferences, store selected products, and improve website functionality.

11. Children's Privacy

Users should only use Zyper Diamond Store in accordance with applicable local laws and the terms of the gaming services they use.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website features, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be available on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your Zyper Diamond Store account, you may contact our support team through the official website.
